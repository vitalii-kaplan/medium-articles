Yes. In a rough historical period, **mobility is one of the highest survival skills**.

But not only physical mobility. The important form is **optionality**.

There are several layers:

1. **Legal mobility**
   Passport, visas, residence permits, clean immigration history, clean tax history, no unresolved legal problems. This is the base layer. Without it, everything else becomes theory.

2. **Professional mobility**
   Skills that can be sold in different countries and markets. Software engineering, AI, data, research, English-language technical writing, consulting. Your background is good for this.

3. **Financial mobility**
   Not keeping all money in one currency, one bank, one country, or one political system. Also not owning only assets that cannot move.

4. **Cultural mobility**
   Ability to function among different people without needing them to share your ideology, language, religion, or historical trauma. This is underrated. Many smart people fail here.

5. **Psychological mobility**
   Not being emotionally chained to a collapsing system, a propaganda narrative, or a personal myth. The ability to say: “This place, institution, party, or identity no longer serves my future.”

The key point: **migration itself is not enough**. Many people move geographically but carry the same dependency structure with them: one employer, one visa, one ethnic bubble, one political obsession, one fragile income source.
